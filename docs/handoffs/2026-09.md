- “El Sendero Perdido” has complete authored text and Nia-specific labels. Trial-story copy is functional placeholder content intended for playtesting.

## Progression and persistence

- There is no XP or character-level system.
- Skills and lifetime coin progress have no ceiling. Achievements unlock at 1 and every multiple of 5 indefinitely.
- Five-block skill bars show progress toward the next milestone while the numeric label shows the absolute total.
- Current-run decisions are keyed by story and chapter. Changing a decision within a chapter replaces its point. Completing a story banks only that story’s choices into permanent character skills and preserves progress in other stories.
- Closing questions award 1, 2, and 3 coins in order. Incorrect answers have unlimited retries and no penalty. `coins` is the spendable wallet; `lifetimeCoins` never decreases when characters are unlocked.
- State persists in `localStorage` under `aventuras-pixel-progress-v3`, with normalization/migration for older saves.
